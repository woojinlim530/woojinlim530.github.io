About Me

I operate at the convergence of Machine Learning Engineering and Large-Scale Data Analytics. My academic foundation in Cognitive Science and Computing Science at SFU bridges psychology, formal linguistics, and philosophy with low-level systems programming. Rather than relying on surface-level intuitions, this interdisciplinary background allows me to analyze complex high-dimensional problems through multiple theoretical lenses and formulate novel, robust solutions.

My engineering philosophy centers on complete transparency and control over internal mechanics. I avoid treating modern frameworks as black boxes; instead, I build from first principles—whether reproducing GPT-2 attention pipelines from scratch in PyTorch, accelerating graph traversals via C++ SIMD intrinsics and cache-conscious designs, or engineering high-throughput spatial data processing pipelines.

Currently, my priorities center on distributed data processing, foundational language model architectures, and data-driven modeling. I bring together the rigorous discipline of low-level hardware performance and the creative, macro-level thinking required to scale modern intelligent systems.
